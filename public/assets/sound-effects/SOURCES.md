# Sound effects sources

The recorded assets in this folder are sourced from the CC0 1.0 releases below. Timeline Studio selected, trimmed, gain-matched, and encoded them as short mono MP3 files. The eight original synthesized sounds are generated by the editor.

- [Meta Open Sound](https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound/tree/251c0be57d5d1b3242b91f53e0cde62ab1cb42d9/systems/instruments), Meta Creative Audio. Only the CC0 instrument collections are included.
- Kenney Vleugels: [Voiceover Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/voiceover-pack), [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio), [Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio), [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), and [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds). The source recordings were retrieved through the [pinned mirror](https://github.com/Mcamento8/open-game-sfx-index/tree/34bbe8b525b5ceff3804915ea745532af11f6055/audio).
- [Sound Effects Pack](https://opengameart.org/node/86682), OwlishMedia.
- [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
